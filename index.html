Description

Michael Roberts Estate Agents are delighted to introduce to the market this beautifully presented period property which is located on the highly sought-after North Circular Road and properties on this stretch of Road rarely frequent the market. "Snugboro" is an imposing four bed detached residence which is set off the road on a leafy elevated and private site. This fantastic period residence boasts large and spacious front and back gardens, two well-proportioned reception rooms with feature fireplaces and both having large bay windows which allows for an abundance of natural light to fill the bright and airy living spaces. There's has access to the rear offering secure off-street parking and also has the added benefit of a detached self-contained mews dwelling c. 310sq.ft.

The property stands adjacent to the cities most respected Secondary Schools, Ard Scoil Ris and is within strolling distance of a number of National Schools. Other amenities include the Limerick Lawn Tennis Club, Shelbourne Park, The Limerick Institute of Technology and the city centre is within 1km. There's easy access to Shannon Airport and major Road Networks.

Garden - Standing 20 meters off the road on an elevated site with beautifully mature landscaped gardens. The front of the property boasts immaculate manicured lawns with a paved footpath. The rear garden provides off street parking, landscaped gardens and also boasts fully contained mews dwelling and has a large garden shed.
